The Concept

Serene Drives is a peaceful driving experience designed to help you relax and unwind. Unlike traditional racing games, there are no time limits, no opponents, and no pressure.

Just you, your car, and beautiful procedurally generated landscapes to explore at your own pace.

Features

  • Beautiful 3D environments with dynamic weather and day/night cycles
  • Realistic physics with satisfying driving mechanics
  • Multiple camera angles for different perspectives
  • Customizable car appearance and performance
  • Soothing ambient soundtrack and nature sounds

Technology

Serene Drives is built with Three.js, a powerful JavaScript 3D library that runs directly in your browser without requiring any plugins or downloads.

The game uses advanced rendering techniques including dynamic lighting, fog effects, and realistic materials to create an immersive experience.
